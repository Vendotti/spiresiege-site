Spire Siege: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Spire Siege is a single-player game. We don't collect, store or share any personal data, and we don't track you.

What the game stores

Your game progress (talent points, unlocked talents, high scores) and settings (sound, music, haptics) are saved on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, the same progress is copied to your own iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. We, the developer, can't see or access this data.

Apple services

  • Game Center: if you're signed in, your best round on each map and your achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.
  • In-App Purchases: the optional "Spire Siege Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The game contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services from third parties, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Because we collect no data, the game is suitable for players of all ages from a privacy point of view.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage and delete the data for Spire Siege.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: spiresiege@gmail.com
