Spire Siege: Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email spiresiege@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

I bought Pro but it isn't unlocked

Open Settings in the game (or the Pro screen) and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

My progress didn't sync to my other device

Progress syncs through iCloud. Check that both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account and that iCloud Drive is turned on, then open the game on both devices. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I earn talent points?

You earn 1 talent point for every 5 rounds survived, plus bonus points the first time you reach round 20, beat a boss, or complete a map (reach round 30). Quitting a run still awards points for the rounds you survived.

Can I reset my talents?

Yes. On the Talents screen, tap Reset to get all your points back for free.
